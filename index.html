The Atelier

Where Fabric
Meets Sculpture

In a quiet studio above Melrose Avenue, far from the noise of seasons and trends, Atelier Solitaire practices a slower discipline, the making of a single gown for a single woman, cut to her body and to her moment.

Every commission begins in muslin and ends in silk. Patterns are drafted by hand, draped on the stand, and corrected at the mirror, a conversation between fabric and form that may last two hundred hours before a single seam is closed.

We are a Los Angeles house, and we make dresses for Los Angeles light, for the way silk moves at dusk in the Hills, for the flash of a camera, for rooms where a woman is seen before she is heard.

We accept a small number of commissions each year. Each is a collaboration; each, in the end, belongs to one evening and one woman alone.

Romilly Law

Founder & Creative Director. 2021, Los Angeles

From Muslin to Mirror

The Art of Creation

01

Consultation

A private conversation in the atelier, or over tea. We speak of the occasion, the woman, and the life the dress must live.

02

Design

Hand-drawn sketches and a curated table of silks, tulles, and embroideries from the mills of Lyon and Como.

03

Fittings

Three private fittings across eight to twelve weeks, as the gown is sculpted patiently to your movement.

04

Delivery

The final pressing, the final mirror. Your gown leaves the atelier in a handmade box, ready for its evening.

The gown did not enter the room before me — it arrived with me, as if we had been made together. Which, of course, we had.

A. Laurent — Oscar Night, 2024

Three fittings, two hundred hours, one dress. I have never felt so precisely understood by a garment.

M. Chen — Met Gala, 2023

She listened longer than she measured. The dress knew things about me I had not said aloud.

R. Duval — Wedding at San Ysidro Ranch, 2025

Founder & Chief Executive

Romilly Law

Romilly aesthetic foundation stems from the rigorous training she received at Parsons School of Design, where she was immersed in womenswear tailoring, visual styling, and a sophisticated commercial perspective. In 2021, she returned to Los Angeles and co-founded this unconventional creative studio with her close friend Scarlett Martin. Her design narratives and business acumen have resonated with the evolving industry, garnering her numerous accolades and awards. As a multi-faceted creator, irector, fashion strategist, and industry pioneer, she is redefining the contemporary fashion landscape with her unique strategic vision.
